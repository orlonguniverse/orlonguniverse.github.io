Behind the creation

THE CREATOR

Callum Macann is the creator of The Orlong Universe, an interconnected science-fiction and fantasy saga spanning multiple worlds, characters and generations.

Growing up in Bundaberg, Callum filled his free time immersing himself in music, chess, cinema and stories. On April 1st, 2023, he decided he wanted to create a world of his own.

By combining his interests in dystopia, genetics and philosophy, the Orlong series began to emerge. He hasn't turned back since.

Orlong is more than a book series. It challenges ideas about society and explores realities that might be better than our own — and others that certainly wouldn't be.
