A SIMPLE PLACE TO BEGIN

YOUR FIRST VISIT

You don't need to learn the whole website before you start. Follow these steps and discover the extra features only when you want them.

1

Open the Reading Room

Choose Enter the Reading Room. You'll find the current chapter, the chapter directory and your reading controls in one place. A free confirmed reader account is required to open released chapters.

2

Set up your reading

Adjust text size, font and page appearance whenever you like. Focus Mode hides distractions so you can concentrate on the story.

3

Join in when you're ready

A free reader account unlocks your reader profile and community features. You can explore discussions, achievements and collectibles as you go.
