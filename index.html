Behind the universe

THE CREATOR

Callum Macann is the creator of The Orlong Universe, an interconnected science-fiction and fantasy saga spanning multiple worlds, characters and generations.

Growing up in Bundbaerg, Callum filled his free time emersing himself in the culture of music, chess cinema, and stories. On April 1st, 2023, however, he decided he wanted to create a world of his own. By combining such interests as dystopia, genetics, and philosophy, the Orlong series has emerged. And he hasn't turned back since.

Orlong is more than a book series. It is challenging the ideas of society and letting the world unfold into some realities that would be better off than those of the present. And others that wouldn't be.

This is where the stories connect, the worlds unfold, and extraordinary destinies begin.
