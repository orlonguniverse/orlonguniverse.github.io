CARD-UNLOCKED LORE LIBRARY

THE EDEN ARCHIVE

Create lore records and connect one or more required cards. Readers only receive a published entry after collecting every connected card and passing its optional chapter release gate.

HOW UNLOCKS WORK

CARDS ARE THE KEYS

Every entry needs at least one required card. If you connect three cards, a reader must own all three before the entry appears.

An optional chapter gate adds another layer: even readers with every required card cannot access the entry until the selected chapter has been released and they have permission to read it.

Unpublished entries remain hidden. Drafts and locked entry details are never exposed through the reader-facing archive request.

SERIES & READING ROOM CONTROL

BOOK CATALOGUE

Add future books before launch, choose their series theme, control whether they appear publicly, and decide whether they appear in the Reading Room book switcher.

HOW BOOK LAUNCHES WORK

Keep future books hidden until you're ready.

  1. Add the book details and its Reading Room URL.
  2. Leave publication turned off while you prepare the page.
  3. Publish when ready. When you launch a second book, enable the Reading Room switcher for both that book and the current book.
  4. Every new book receives its own Reading Room and chapter guide automatically. Its chapters and manuscripts are managed separately.

Removing a catalogue entry never deletes website files, chapters or reader progress. The live Trials of Eden entry is protected and can be hidden instead.
