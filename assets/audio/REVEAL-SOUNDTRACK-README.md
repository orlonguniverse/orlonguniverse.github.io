# Card reveal soundtrack

Upload one continuous, full-length cinematic soundtrack here as:

`reveal-soundtrack.mp3`

This single track replaces the separate awakening, energy, materialisation, flip, rarity and impact sound effects during the card reveal. The reveal sequence will play this soundtrack once from the beginning while the visual stages unfold.

Keep the original creator, source URL and licence information for your records. The reveal soundtrack is separate from the optional sitewide `background-music.mp3`.
