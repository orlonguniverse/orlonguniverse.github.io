NEW TO THE READING ROOM?

Everything you need to start reading.

The chapter is the main event. These quick tips explain the rest — and you can close this guide whenever you're ready.

01 · CHOOSE A CHAPTERUse the chapter list on the left. Only released chapters can be opened.
02 · MAKE IT COMFORTABLEUse A− / A+ for text size. Open Reading Options for fonts, page colours and Focus Mode.
03 · THE READING COMPANIONSelect highlighted names or terms to see short reference notes. It is there if you want extra context; you can ignore it and simply read.
04 · YOUR PROGRESSUse the chapter milestone button below the manuscript to mark a chapter complete. Sign in when prompted to save milestones, react or join discussions.
