THE READER GUIDE

Everything you need to know about reading The Trials of Eden and using the optional features around it. You don't need to use everything — the story always comes first.

Ready to begin?

Open the Reading Room to read the released chapters. You can come back to this guide whenever you need it.

01 · THE STORY

Reading The Trials of Eden

Chapter directory

Use the chapter list on the left of the Reading Room to move between released chapters. Upcoming chapters may appear as Coming Soon, but cannot be opened yet.

Reading controls

Use A− and A+ to adjust text size. In Reading Options, change the font, page appearance and other preferences. Focus Mode centres the manuscript and hides distractions.

Reading Companion

Some names and terms are highlighted. Select one to see a short reference note. The Companion is optional — you can ignore it and read straight through.

Chapter milestones

At the end of a chapter, use Mark Chapter Complete to save the milestone to your reader account. You can also leave a reaction or join the chapter discussion when signed in.

02 · YOUR ACCOUNT

Why create a free reader account?

A free confirmed account is required to open released chapters and lets the website associate your reading milestones, reactions, comments, profile and collection with you. Email confirmation is required when signing up. Your email address is not displayed publicly.

Your Statistics page is your personal dashboard. It brings together your profile, reading progress, community activity, achievements, collectible binder and trading tools.

03 · OPTIONAL EXTRAS

Explore the reader features

My Collection

Your personal binder of cards you have discovered or received. You can manage duplicates and choose whether to sell extra copies.

Card Collection

The released catalogue. Browse available cards and see which ones you have yet to discover.

Trading Post & Wanted Board

Offer exact card copies, negotiate multi-card exchanges and Xanth, or publish a request for a card you want.

Marketplace & Mystery Packs

Use Xanth, the website's fictional currency, in the rotating daily shop and Mystery Vault. Pack odds and prices are shown before purchase.

Notifications

Check your reader inbox for trade activity, achievements and event updates. You can mark notifications read individually or together.

Events & Early Access

Events are community activities and announcements. Early Access is a separate private area and requires an invitation — neither is needed to read the book.

04 · QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to collect cards or trade?

No. Collecting, trading, Mystery Packs, events and other community features are optional extras. You can simply read the book.

Do I need to pay to use the website?

No paid membership or real-money purchase is required for the reader features described here. Xanth is an in-world game currency, not a payment method.

Why can't I open an upcoming chapter?

Chapters become available when they are released. Upcoming entries are shown for orientation, but only released chapters can be opened.

Can other readers see my email address?

No. Your email is used for account access and is not displayed publicly. Use a display name for community interactions.

Where do I go if something doesn't work?

Refresh the page and try again. If the problem continues, contact the creator using the email link below and include the page you were on and what you were trying to do. Never send your password.
